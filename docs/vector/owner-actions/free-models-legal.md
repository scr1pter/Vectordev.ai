## Proposed privacy paragraph

When you connect your own free OpenRouter account and choose an eligible model under Free models inside of Vector, your prompts, source-code context, tool results, and model responses are processed by OpenRouter and the downstream provider serving that request. Model requests go directly to OpenRouter using your provider connection or environment key; they do not use Vector's shared proxy or allowance. The selection is limited to the explicit free variants on Vector's allowlist of models and the providers that serve them, with online, tool-capable, zero-price endpoints, matched by model ID and endpoint tag. These providers may retain prompts and outputs under their own policies. Every request asks OpenRouter to deny data collection, which excludes endpoints OpenRouter records as training on prompts. The allowlist is on the Models and providers documentation page, and the public catalog at `/api/free-models/models` names the provider serving each model. (The app's model picker does not yet show the serving provider.) This relies on the providers' published policies and OpenRouter's records and routing controls, not an independent Vector audit. It does not promise zero retention by any provider, local Vector history, OpenRouter account logging, other providers or external services. Review those settings and policies before sending confidential information. Personal access works while the shared service remains disabled.

## Proposed terms paragraph

Free models inside of Vector uses eligible free model access offered by OpenRouter and downstream providers through your own account. Model availability and account limits can change. The guarded route must stop when eligible zero-price endpoints or the free allowance are unavailable, without switching to paid models or providers. Keep the connected account free: do not purchase credits, add a payment method, enable automatic top-ups, attach paid upstream BYOK credentials, or enable default or enforced paid plugins. Wait for the allowance to reset rather than buying more capacity. Connecting the account remains subject to OpenRouter's terms. Other provider connections, external agents, and separately enabled services are outside this free route.

## Decisions and exact claims to review

- Describe the selection as allowlisted models and providers with enforced routing controls: providers may retain prompts and outputs under their own policies, and Vector requests that they are not used for training. Never call these models zero data retention. Do not call the historical shared table current, claim independent manual verification, or extend the endpoint restriction to all logs and local history.
