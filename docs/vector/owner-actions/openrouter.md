# OpenRouter free access — owner actions

**Superseded on 17 October 2026.** The owner decided that the free-model budget on Vector's own OpenRouter account is the free path for every signed-in Vector user ("i want it so that the vector app is connected to my openrouter account no matter who uses the free models"), and bought $10 of OpenRouter credits for it; see [Free models of 17 October 2026: owner steps](#free-models-of-17-october-2026-owner-steps). The rest of this paragraph and the sections after those steps are the history.

Decision (1.99.99): use each user's own free OpenRouter account for the upcoming **1.99.99** release. Personal free-model discovery and setup work independently of `FREE_MODELS_ENABLED=false`. Leave the shared service off; do not add a shared key, activate KV for this feature, or purchase credits. The currently downloadable desktop **1.99.8** does not include this guarded setup.

No account was created, terms accepted, purchase made, key read, privacy setting changed or message sent by the implementation agent. Account-specific endpoint viability has **not** been verified. The candidate snapshot below was checked on 25 September 2026; it is not a promise of current availability.

On 2 October 2026, OpenRouter's [pricing page](https://openrouter.ai/pricing) listed Data Policy-Based Routing as unavailable on Free, while its [per-request ZDR documentation](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/features/zdr#per-request-zdr-enforcement) described `provider.zdr` without an account-tier qualification. These sources do not establish whether an unfunded account can use Vector's `data_collection: "deny"` and provider-list constraints. (Since 15 October 2026 included models no longer request `zdr: true`; see below.) Public endpoint metadata and passing fixture tests do not resolve that entitlement question. Keep the guards intact and treat rejected requests as unavailable; purchasing credits or upgrading is not an acceptable workaround.

## Free models of 17 October 2026: owner steps

The owner decided on 17 October 2026 to offer every free OpenRouter model, including models whose providers may train on prompts, behind the label "May train on your prompts" and a one-time consent; see [FREE-MODELS.md](../FREE-MODELS.md#every-free-model-with-its-data-policy-owner-decision-17-october-2026). Code cannot do the steps below. Nothing here was done by the implementation agent: no account settings were changed, no key was read and no authenticated or paid call was made.

**Why production shows no free models today.** On 17 October 2026 at about 23:40 UTC, `GET https://vectordev.ai/api/free-models/models` answered 503 because the catalog was never written under the storage key in use. The import fix (4f03b239b) landed after that day's 03:17 UTC cron had already failed. After this branch deploys, the key is `vector:free-models:catalog:v4:policy`, which is empty until the refresh runs, and `/models` then names the case (`FREE_MODELS_CATALOG_MISSING`).

In the OpenRouter account behind `OPENROUTER_FREE_API_KEY`, at [openrouter.ai/settings/privacy](https://openrouter.ai/settings/privacy). OpenRouter's documentation says the account settings page sets "whether you would like to allow routing to providers that may train on your data", with separate settings for paid and free models. The exact toggle labels below come from third-party descriptions, because the settings page needs sign-in; match them to what the page shows.

1. **Free models: allow providers that may train on your data — turn ON.** Without it OpenRouter never routes to Poolside, Liquid or Thinking Machines, so the shared catalog keeps only no-training models (the account's `/models/user` list leaves them out). Every request still narrows routing: no-training models, and every user who has not accepted, keep `data_collection: "deny"`.
2. **Paid models: allow providers that may train — keep OFF.** Codium is paid and must not change.
3. **Providers that may publish prompts — keep OFF.** No free endpoint publishes prompts today, and the consent covers training, not publication.
4. **"ZDR endpoints only" — keep OFF.** With it on, every free model is refused (`{"enabled":false,"count":0}`). Codium already sends `zdr: true` on each request, so it does not need the account-wide setting.
5. **Ignored and allowed providers.** Do not ignore `cohere`, `atlas-cloud`, `phala`, `poolside`, `liquid`, `thinkingmachines`, `nvidia`, `recursive` or `google-ai-studio`. Since 23 October 2026 the shared catalog offers every free model the account's own list (`/models/user`) includes, new providers too, so keep no allowed-providers list: one hides every provider it does not name, and OpenRouter answers 404 when the account list and a request's `only` list do not overlap. **If `nvidia` was added to the ignored providers as the router guard this step used to suggest, remove it:** NVIDIA's models are offered since the owner's decision of 23 October 2026, and an ignored provider keeps them out of the account's list. `google-ai-studio` serves Gemma on Vector’s account too; those models carry a US only tag, with no location check. The refresh logs which offered free models the account's list leaves out ("Free models the OpenRouter account's own list leaves out: …" in the Vercel function log of `/api/free-models/refresh`); any model named there is hidden by a setting in this list or step 1.
6. **Prompt logging and OpenRouter's own use of inputs — keep OFF.**
7. **Credits and balance.** The account has bought $10 of credits (owner, 17 October 2026), so its free tier is 1,000 requests a day instead of 50; OpenRouter sets the tier by credits purchased over the account's life, so it holds as the balance is spent. The limit stays 20 a minute across all the account's keys. **Never let the balance go negative:** OpenRouter answers HTTP 402 to free-model requests from an account with a negative balance, which stops the free-model budget for every user. Vector's requests send `max_price: 0` and disable paid plugins, so free use spends nothing. Keep automatic top-ups, paid upstream BYOK keys and default or enforced paid plugins off, use this account and its key for nothing else, and check the balance on [openrouter.ai/settings/credits](https://openrouter.ai/settings/credits) after deploys.

Then, after this branch deploys:

8. **Run the refresh.** In Vercel open the project → Settings → Cron Jobs → **Run** on `/api/free-models/refresh`, or send `GET https://vectordev.ai/api/free-models/refresh` with `Authorization: Bearer <CRON_SECRET>`. The answer says which case you are in:
   - `{"enabled":true,"count":16}` (more or fewer as models come and go): working. On the 24 October 2026 snapshot those are North Mini Code, Dots3-Note Preview, Gemma 4 26B and 31B, and Xing4.0 (no training), LFM2.5-2.6B, Nemotron 3 Nano Omni, Nemotron 3 Ultra, Nemotron 3.5 Lightning, Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, Laguna S 2.1, Laguna XS 2.1, Flint, Inkling Small and Inkling (may train), and the Free Models Router (labelled as one that may train); Nemotron 3 Super joins once its endpoint answers again.    - `{"enabled":false,"count":0}`: OpenRouter's account list allows nothing, or only the Free Models Router, which on its own never turns the section on; recheck steps 1, 4 and 5.
   - 503 `FREE_MODELS_CATALOG_UNAVAILABLE`: an OpenRouter read failed, probably a bad or revoked `OPENROUTER_FREE_API_KEY`.

   - 503 `FREE_MODELS_UNAVAILABLE`: no key environment variable is set.

9. **Check both catalogs.** `GET https://vectordev.ai/api/free-models/models` (what 1.99.108 and CLI 1.99.99 see) should list no-training models only; `GET https://vectordev.ai/api/free-models/models?disclosure=may-train-v1` adds the may-train ones, each provider with `"dataPolicy"`.

11. **Try one harmless prompt** from a signed-in 1.99.109 build to one no-training model, one may-train model and the Free Models Router (accepting the notice), and confirm each answers and the balance is unchanged. A refusal shows "temporarily unavailable" and never falls back to a paid endpoint. Still unverified, because it needs a logged-in call: whether the account's free tier can use `data_collection`, `only` and `max_price` routing at all (OpenRouter's pricing table lists Data Policy-Based Routing as not on the Free plan; a credit purchase may change that), and whether the Free Models Router honours `provider.ignore`. ## No-training policy: owner steps (15 October 2026, superseded on 17 October)

Steps 1, 3 and 4 below still apply. Step 2 changed: free endpoints that may train must now be allowed on the account (see above).

The included-models policy changed from zero data retention to no training: providers may retain prompts under their own policies but must not train on them. Included and personal requests send `data_collection: "deny"`, the allowlisted provider `only` list, `require_parameters: true` and a zero `max_price`, and no longer send `zdr: true`. The allowlist names each model with the provider that may serve it: Cohere for North Mini Code and AtlasCloud for Dots3-Note Preview (see [No-training evidence per model](#no-training-evidence-per-model)). NovitaAI and ModelRun serve no free model today and were dropped until they are re-reviewed. Poolside is excluded because OpenRouter's free Laguna pages say free use may train on inputs. Codium is unchanged and still requests `zdr: true` on every request, so an account-wide ZDR setting is not needed for it.

In the OpenRouter account behind `OPENROUTER_FREE_API_KEY` (its privacy and provider-routing settings; the labels below are approximate):

1. Turn **off** the account-wide "ZDR endpoints only" restriction. With it on, OpenRouter refuses every included model, because none of their endpoints is zero data retention.
2. Keep training **disabled** for both free and paid endpoints ("may train on inputs" off), and keep "may publish prompts" off. Each request already sends `data_collection: "deny"`; these account settings are a second guard.
3. Keep prompt and input/output logging **off**, and keep OpenRouter's own use of inputs off.
4. Make sure Cohere and AtlasCloud are not ignored providers. If the account has an allowed-providers list, it must include `cohere` and `atlas-cloud`; OpenRouter answers 404 when an account allowlist and a request-level `only` list do not overlap.
5. Keep the account unfunded: no credit purchase, payment method, automatic top-up or BYOK provider key.

Before deploying, complete the per-model confirmations in [No-training evidence per model](#no-training-evidence-per-model). After deploying, force a refresh instead of waiting for the 03:17 UTC cron: in Vercel open the project → Settings → Cron Jobs and choose **Run** on `/api/free-models/refresh`, or send `GET https://vectordev.ai/api/free-models/refresh` with the header `Authorization: Bearer <CRON_SECRET>`. A good answer is `{"enabled":true,"updatedAt":…,"count":2}`. Then `https://vectordev.ai/api/free-models/models` should list `cohere/north-mini-code:free` and `dots-studio/dots-3-note-preview:free`. If nothing qualifies, the refresh publishes `{"enabled":false}` and the app hides the section. Finally send one harmless prompt to each model from a signed-in desktop and confirm it answers and the account balance is unchanged. A refusal shows "temporarily unavailable" and never falls back to a paid endpoint; if that happens, recheck steps 1 to 4. One unreadable endpoint list drops only that model from a refresh; a failed `/models` or `/models/user` read keeps the stored catalog and answers 503.

## Selected setup: personal free accounts

1. Each user connects their own OpenRouter account through **Connect OpenRouter** in Vector, saves their API key in Settings → Providers, or sets `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` in the engine environment. A project-only `provider.openrouter.options.apiKey` value is insufficient for guarded free access; migrate it to the provider connection or environment without committing or printing it. A shared Vector account or shared OpenRouter credential is not needed for model routing. See [connection instructions](../FREE-MODELS.md).
2. Keep that OpenRouter account free: no credit purchase, payment method, automatic top-ups, paid upstream provider keys in OpenRouter's BYOK settings, or default/enforced paid plugins. Check the account's settings directly; Vector cannot remove account-level payment arrangements on the user's behalf.
3. Select an eligible model under **Free models inside of Vector → Your OpenRouter account**. The scope is the allowlisted explicit `:free` models with online, tool-capable, zero-price endpoints from the provider allowlisted for each, read from each model's public `/api/v1/models/{author}/{slug}/endpoints` list. Discovery matches model ID and endpoint tag. It is not a list of every free model.
4. Keep zero-price routing, `data_collection: "deny"`, the allowlisted provider list, and the protections against paid plugins and paid model/provider fallbacks. Missing eligibility, an unavailable endpoint, and exhausted limits must stop the request. Do not relax the constraints to make a request succeed.
5. OpenRouter currently lists up to 50 requests/day and 20 requests/minute without buying credits. A coding task can require many requests. Wait for a reset when limits are reached; do not purchase a higher budget or create accounts to evade limits. See the [current upstream policy](https://openrouter.ai/docs/api_reference/limits).
6. Review the user's OpenRouter privacy settings and downstream policies. Providers may retain prompts and outputs under their own policies; Vector requests that they are not used for training. This routing restriction does not cover Vector's local history, OpenRouter account logging, or every external tool/service. An account restricted to ZDR endpoints only gets no included models, because none of their endpoints is zero data retention. Keep other independently configured providers/tools distinct from free model access.

Before describing personal access as verified, the owner must exercise the reviewed candidate with their own unfunded account and an eligible model. Confirm that a harmless prompt succeeds under the unchanged privacy and zero-price constraints, and that the account balance is unchanged. Report only the model ID, success or rejection, and the balance result; keep credentials and request headers private. A rejection keeps live availability unverified and must not trigger paid routing, a credit purchase, or relaxed privacy settings.
