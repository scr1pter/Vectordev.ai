## Obligations for A22 and later

- **A22 (C2, C3).** Stamp `billing` where usage is recorded. Route accounting by the flag stored on the record that
  the projector adds and later subtracts, as label plan §4.1 and §4.2 say: `usage()` reads the step-finish part's
  `billing`, `messageUsage()` the V2 assistant or compaction row's, and the Step.Ended and compaction handlers their
  event's. A record without the flag counts as an ordinary step on both sides. Never route accounting through
  `Billing.of`: its provider-id fallback is for display. There is no backfill (§4.2), so a Codium step recorded
  before A22 was added as an ordinary step; read as Codium when it is reverted or removed (`projector.ts:395,413,438`
  and the V2 revert at `:644`), it would subtract a Codium step that was never added. That leaves `codium_steps`, and
  `subagent_codium_steps` on every ancestor, at −1 with the step's tokens still in the totals, and the V1 and engine
  `Info` then fail their `NonNegativeInt` encoding on every `session.get` and `session.list` that includes the
  session (checked: encoding `SessionV1.SessionInfo` with `codiumSteps: -1` fails, as `unpricedSteps: -1` already
  does). The contract's provider-id check on AncillaryUsage (§4.2) is safe, because nothing subtracts ancillary
  usage. Two cases follow:
  - The local `updatePart` endpoint (`packages/engine/src/server/routes/instance/httpapi/groups/session.ts`, payload
    `SessionV1.Part`) lets a client rewrite a part's `billing`, as it already can `cost`, `tokens` and `unpriced`
    (display-only, D-2). `PartUpdated` subtracts the stored part before it adds the new one, so a rewrite moves that
    step between the Codium and own counts and cannot drive either below 0.
  - Fork (`Session.fork`, `packages/engine/src/session/session.ts:919-970`) copies each step-finish part with its
    cost, tokens and `unpriced` cleared, so the copy carries no spend. It must clear `billing` too, or every copied
    Codium step adds a Codium step to the fork. Routing by the message's flag instead would not avoid this: the
    cloned message keeps its `billing`, and the `Session.updateMessage` stamp would set it again.

  Symmetry, not a clamp, keeps the counts at 0 or more; a clamp would hide a wrong route and still leave the old
  step's tokens in the totals. Tests: in `session-projector.test.ts`, reverting and removing a step recorded without
  the flag on a `vector-plan` message leave `codium_steps` at 0 and take its tokens out; in
  `packages/engine/test/session/session.test.ts`, forking a session with a Codium step adds none to the fork. Map
  `codiumSteps`/`subagentCodiumSteps` in `fromRow`/`toRow`, `info.ts`, `archive.ts`, and add both columns to the
  projector's `SessionV1.Event.Updated` strip list.

- **A23 (C4, C5).** Fill the guard's `codiumSteps` (never in its `unpricedSteps`), copy it in the V2 spend handler and
  the desktop's `combineSpend`, set it on `BudgetStopError`; fill the usage summary's Codium counts and leave Codium
  tokens out of every token total at the same time, as the field comments say.
- **A26 (C11).** Define the combined review cost kind for Codium mixed with priced or plan passes. Today
  `combineCostKinds` gives `"unknown"` for that mix, and `costWording` renders `"unknown"` as "cost unknown … (tokens)",
  which label plan §2 and §3.4 forbid for Codium work ("$0.40 + Codium").
- **Version skew.** `Billing.of` falls back to `providerID === "vector-plan"`; a user-configured custom provider with
  that id would read as Codium. Provider policy and the Codium plugin own the id, so the risk is low.
- The new `billing` key on the shared `ancillaryUsage` fields also widens the read-only `AncillaryUsageV1` decoder;
  harmless (V1 is never written, excess keys were already ignored).
