Send a message to another agent working with you in this shared workspace.

Use this when you genuinely need a teammate to know something or to answer you:

- You changed something they depend on (a shared type, an interface, a route).
- You are about to edit a file they are also working in, and want to avoid a collision.
- You need a decision or an answer only they can give.
- You finished a piece they are blocked on.

Do not use it to narrate routine progress. Vector already broadcasts a summary of
your work when your turn completes, so a message that only says what you did is
noise for everyone else.

Address one teammate by name when the message is for them specifically. Leave the
recipient out to reach everyone on the team.

Messages are delivered at the start of the recipient's next turn, not instantly,
so do not wait on a reply within this turn. Say what you need, then continue with
work you can do without the answer.
