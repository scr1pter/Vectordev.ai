How heat pumps cut heating bills in cold climates

By Mara Lindqvist, 14 March 2026

The study followed 412 homes through two winters. Homes that replaced an oil boiler with an air-source heat pump used 61 percent less delivered energy for heating, measured at the meter.

What the meters showed

The seasonal coefficient of performance averaged 2.9 across the sample, and stayed above 2.0 on days when the outdoor temperature fell below minus 15 degrees Celsius.

  • Median annual saving: 640 euros per home.
  • Homes with poor insulation saved less, about 31 percent.

Method

Readings came from smart meters every fifteen minutes. The full data set is published under an open licence at the study's data page.
